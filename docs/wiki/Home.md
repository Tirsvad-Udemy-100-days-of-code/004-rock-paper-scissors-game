# Rock Paper Scissors Game

A beginner-friendly Python program to play Rock, Paper, Scissors against the computer. It is Day 4 of Udemy's *100 Days of Code™: The Complete Python Pro Bootcamp*, built and documented to the Software Quality Assurance (SQA) and Quality Control (QC) framework.

You choose `0` (Rock), `1` (Paper) or `2` (Scissors); the computer picks at random; both choices are drawn as ASCII art and the winner is announced.
